Privacy Policy

Niramaya Acupressure Therapy respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the information you provide while using our website, consultation services and related communication channels.

Information We Collect

Depending on the service you use, we may collect:

  • To process and reconcile payments, refunds and cancellations
