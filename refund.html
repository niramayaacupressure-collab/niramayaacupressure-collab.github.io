Refund & Cancellation Policy

Cancellation More Than 24 Hours Before Consultation

The customer is eligible for a full refund.

Cancellation Within 24 Hours

The payment is non-refundable. However, the customer may request one rescheduling opportunity.

No-Show

If the customer does not attend the scheduled consultation without prior arrangement, the payment is non-refundable.

Cancellation by Niramaya

If Niramaya cancels the consultation or is unable to provide the service, the customer may choose between a full refund or rescheduling.

After the Consultation Begins

Once the consultation has begun, the service is considered commenced and the payment is non-refundable.

Duplicate or Erroneous Payment

If a customer believes that a payment has been duplicated or incorrectly charged, they should contact Niramaya with the transaction details so that the payment can be verified and, where applicable, corrected or refunded.
